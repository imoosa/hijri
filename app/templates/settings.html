Location Settings

This is stored in your browser session only (not a real user account system). Prayer times, qibla direction, and the calendar's location line all use this.

Type a place name and hit Find (or press Enter in that field) to look it up -- it fills in coordinates and expands the name to "City, State, Country" using OpenStreetMap. "Use my current location" does the same via your device's GPS instead. Neither submits automatically -- review the fields, then hit Save. Timezone is looked up from the real timezone boundary for that location (correct for non-standard offsets like India's UTC+5:30), not guessed from longitude -- but it's still worth a glance before saving.
